About PKP

Pharma Knowledge Pathsala (PKP) is an educational initiative aimed at helping pharmacy learners understand their subjects and prepare for pharmacist competitive exams through structured, accessible teaching.

Our goal is to make pharmacy learning simpler, practical and affordable.

Why Choose PKP?

Concept-focused teaching
Exam-oriented practice
